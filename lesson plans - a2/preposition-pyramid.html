Preposition Pyramid

In Spanish, one word often does the job: en julio, en la mesa, en casa. In English we need three: in July, on the table, at home. Learn when to use each one for time and place.

ON

TIME

  • on 9 July · dates
  • on my birthday · special days
  • on Friday night · day + part of day

PLACE

  • on the table · surfaces
  • on the wall · surfaces
  • on Corrientes Avenue · streets

AT

Small · exact · a point

TIME

  • at 7 o'clock · clock times
  • at lunchtime · exact moments
  • at night · fixed phrase
  • at Christmas · holiday periods

PLACE

  • at home, at work, at school
  • at the door · points
  • at the bus stop · points
  • at 25 Main Street · full addresses

⚠ WATCH OUT

  • in the morning, in the evening, but at night.
  • Day + part of the day uses on: on Monday morning, not in Monday morning.
  • No preposition with this, next, last, every: next Monday, every day, not on next Monday.
  • Car and taxi use in; bus, train and plane use on.

    My Perfect Saturday

    On Saturday, I get up at nine o'clock. I have breakfast in the kitchen. In the morning, I play football in the park with my friends. At one o'clock, we have lunch at my grandmother's house. In the afternoon, I read a book on the sofa. At night, I watch a film at home.

    ▸ TEACHER NOTES

    About 45 minutes. Suggested timing: pyramid 10, sort it 8, fill the gap 10, talk 7, boss 10.

    • Spanish speakers use en for all three, so expect: “in Monday”, “in the bus”, “in home”, “at the morning”, “in the night”.
    • Dates are tricky: in May but on 5 May. The day wins.
    • At the weekend (UK) and on the weekend (US) are both correct. Accept either.
    • In the boss task the tracker only counts the words. Check each phrase in “Your phrases” with the student.
    • Next lesson: more prepositions of place (under, next to, between, behind, in front of).
